Health benefits

The health benefits of Salsa are boundless. With lively music, the high tempo has great results for cardio. Balance is a key element when doing all that spinning. It'd help to also have a good pair of shoes.

Mental health

Salsa, as well as many other forms of dance is reported to be great for mental health. Some sources quote improvement to mood as well as memory.

Footwear

Cuban salsa is the purest most authentic form of salsa one can do, with no specific form of footwear required. However if you want to get into the style you can wear traditional dancing heels.
